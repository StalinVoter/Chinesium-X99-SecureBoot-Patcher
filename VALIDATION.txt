X99 Secureboot Patcher v0.9 validation
======================================

Regression suite:
  42 / 42 PASS

Coverage includes
-----------------
- AMI Secure Boot target discovery and structure validation;
- exact validated update-payload hashes and certificate content;
- PK auto-planning for AMI test PK vs production/OEM PK;
- KEK/db 2023 certificate planning;
- missing/duplicate/ambiguous target rejection;
- optional/preserved dbx behavior in backend verification;
- firmware-volume mutation-boundary checks;
- input-size and output-size preservation;
- replacement re-extraction matching exact update-payload bytes;
- silent FIT/microcode rebuild safeguard;
- selective repair of rebuild-induced displacement only;
- preservation of a pre-existing stale relationship rather than converting it
  into a general FIT repair operation;
- ambiguity rejection;
- GUI regression asserting no FIT-facing wording or controls.

Board/firmware corpus
---------------------
The generic Secure Boot parser and rebuild safeguards were tested against the
supplied stock/reference firmware from:
- PANDL X99-XD3 compact;
- PANDL/related X99-XD3 classic;
- MACHINIST X99-V9S;
- Qiyida X99-K9S;
- Jginue ARGB X99.

Synthetic rebuild-displacement tests prove that on each supplied family, a new
post-rebuild Type-1 pointer displacement can be mapped back to the exact same
microcode identity. PASS inputs return to their original valid relationship;
pre-existing STALE inputs return to their original stale relationship rather
than being globally repaired.

The application does not claim universal compatibility with every firmware
marketed as X99. Structurally unfamiliar or ambiguous layouts fail closed.
